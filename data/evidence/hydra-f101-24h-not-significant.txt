CLINICAL STUDY REPORT - SUMMARY
Report no.: CRX-2026-033
Test facility: Clinirex Skin Research AB, Malmö, Sweden
Sponsor: NordVita ApS
Report date: 9 July 2026

Test product: NordVita Hydra Serum, formula F-101 rev. 3 (batch 26-0611)

Objective: To verify the duration of the hydrating effect after a single application.

Design: Single-centre, randomised, controlled study with one treated and one untreated area on the volar forearms of each participant.

Participants: 30 healthy women and men aged 22-61 with dry skin. 29 completed the study.

Procedure: A single application of 2 mg/cm2 of the test product. Skin surface hydration was measured with a Corneometer CM 825 at baseline and 2, 6, 12 and 24 hours after application under controlled conditions.

Results: Mean change in skin hydration versus baseline, treated area compared with the untreated control area:
- 2 hours: +35 % (p < 0.001)
- 6 hours: +22 % (p < 0.001)
- 12 hours: +12 % (p = 0.03)
- 24 hours: +3 % (p = 0.41, not statistically significant)

Conclusion: A statistically significant increase in skin hydration was observed up to 12 hours after application. At 24 hours, hydration was not significantly different from the untreated control area.
