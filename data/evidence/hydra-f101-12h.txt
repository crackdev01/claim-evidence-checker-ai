CLINICAL STUDY REPORT - SUMMARY
Report no.: DNL-2026-014
Test facility: Dermatest Nordic ApS, Aarhus, Denmark
Sponsor: NordVita ApS
Report date: 14 April 2026

Test product: NordVita Hydra Serum, formula F-101 rev. 3 (batch 26-0412)

Objective: To measure the effect of a single application of the test product on skin surface hydration.

Design: Single-centre, randomised, controlled study. Each participant had one treated test area and one untreated control area on the volar forearms.

Participants: 32 healthy women aged 25-58 with dry to normal skin (baseline Corneometer value below 45 a.u.). All 32 completed the study.

Procedure: A single standardised application of 2 mg/cm2 of the test product was made to the treated area. Skin surface hydration was measured with a Corneometer CM 825 at baseline and 1, 4, 8 and 12 hours after application, after 30 minutes of acclimatisation at 21 ± 1 °C and 50 ± 5 % relative humidity.

Results: Mean increase in skin hydration versus baseline, treated area compared with the untreated control area:
- 1 hour: +41 % (p < 0.001)
- 4 hours: +33 % (p < 0.001)
- 8 hours: +26 % (p < 0.001)
- 12 hours: +19 % (p < 0.01)

Tolerance: No adverse reactions were observed.

Conclusion: Under the conditions of this study, a single application of NordVita Hydra Serum (F-101 rev. 3) significantly increased skin surface hydration for up to 12 hours compared with untreated skin.
